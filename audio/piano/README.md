# Piano samples

Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm (a sampled Yamaha C5 grand), licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
One velocity layer, sampled every minor third from A0 to C8, as MP3 files taken from the
[Tone.js audio repository](https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander).
The app fills in the notes between samples by changing the playback rate.
